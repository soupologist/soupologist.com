so it seems like India is playing meaningful test matches after a while, with the current tour of New Zealand. I remember the last time India toured New Zealand during COVID back in 2020, where we got utterly cooked. So it's actually crazy that it took till 2026 for us to tour there again. And in January of 2027, we have the Border-Gavaskar Trophy with Australia playing 5 matches in India. They're gonna be playing tests till the beginning of March. 

With good times coming up, I wanna take some time to reflect on why I love this format of the game.

One of my favourite things to do is to work in parallel with a day's play. Each day has three sessions, each of around 2 hours, and spaced by breaks: 40min lunch and 20min tea (we're not talking about Day/Night test matches in this case). So if you follow along, you get 6 solid hours in, with good rest in between as well. For matches in India, they start at 9:30AM and wrap up at around 5PM which is literally perfect. I think I started doing this during JEE prep actually, so around 2020 I'd say is the time I developed this liking with test cricket.

I don't think there's many sports out there that are played as long as test cricket. Each match takes 5 days, maybe around 30 hours but it's very variable. The great thing about it spanning over multiple days is the fact that you can always makeup for a bad day with a really good day.

Take the current match for example: Day 1 wasn't ideal for India with New Zealand capitalising with a huge partnership and posting around 250 runs at the end of the day. But on Day 2, India bowled out NZ and scored around 150 runs losing only 1 wicket. And anything can happen on the next day.
