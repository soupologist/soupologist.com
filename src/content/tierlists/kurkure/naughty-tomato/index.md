I think this flavour is overhated by the general public. I used to really like this flavour when I was a kid, and that nostalgia still remains with me, despite me acknowledging the societal impact of the other more notable flavours like Masala Munch and Chilli Chatka. 

People's main issue with this flavour is that fact that it's so sweet but I don't think it's that prominent.
