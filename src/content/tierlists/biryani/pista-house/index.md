Pista House was a classic that we used to order back in BITS Hyderabad Campus, simply because it was the closest proper biryani available to us while ordering. 

My complaint would be that I'm quite reliant on the salan to provide the flavour because the chicken doesn't really have a strong flavour, it just provides the texture. It's cooked very well, don't get me wrong, but just having the rice and chicken doesn't really give you the hit.
