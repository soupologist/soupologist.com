## A clip with the song attached

`track` accepts any of three formats, so paste whatever Spotify hands you:
the share URL (`https://open.spotify.com/track/…`), the URI
(`spotify:track:…`), or the bare 22-character ID.

<Clip
  src="/videos/events/your-slug/clip-01.mp4"
  poster="/images/events/your-slug/clip-01.jpg"
  caption="He stopped the band and made the whole field sing the second verse."
  track="https://open.spotify.com/track/PASTE_THE_SHARE_LINK"
  trackTitle="Song Name"
  trackArtist="Artist Name"
/>

Clips are `portrait` by default because phone footage usually is. Pass
`orientation="landscape"` or `orientation="square"` when it isn't, and
`silent` for a clip with no audio worth hearing.

<Clip
  src="/videos/events/your-slug/clip-02.mp4"
  orientation="landscape"
  caption="Wide shot of the stage before doors."
  silent
/>
