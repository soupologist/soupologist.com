## looking back

Looking back, the main thing to note is how short this internship was, at barely 2 months. I really only got a first look at the process of building a Springboot backend and a React frontend with Material UI. The only thing new I've learned was the whole process of testing, such as unit testing, integration testing, and performance testing. 

But other than that it was super chill and just flew by. At the time, my concern was primarily to turn down the PPO if offered because I wanted to shoot my shot during placements. The company ended up not offering return offers to any of the interns due to a hiring freeze so that worked out well for me.
