### what even is the company

so Baya's main product is the Network-on-Chip (NoC) that lets components on a chip communicate more efficiently. For example, if your chip has a CPU, GPU, and some memory, the Network we build optimises the flow of data across the grid on the chip in order to improve overall performance and reduce latency. So under the hood, it's all highly optimised C++ code that runs the show.

## benefits of a small company

I do like the fact that I can just walk up to whoever my problem is relevant to and get their thoughts almost instantly, or atleast get them aware of my issue. The office is always very bustling with constant yap from everyone around me, almost always technical discussion but there's a good amount of discussion as well. 

### lot of room in terms of development

Due to how young the company is, a lot of the code is pretty recent too, being written in the past 3 years, probably without AI. So on one hand, we do gotta deal with thousands of lines of code in one file sometimes, but on the other hand there's a lot that can be achieved with Claude and some planning. 

### good balance

Due to the nature of the company, we don't have anything actively deployed and running which can potentially break, which means that there's no on-call practice. Obviously we do handle issues promptly, and keep things hustling throughout the week. But there is a good cycle between the India and US software team which I really like. Everyday morning when I log in, I merge main to incorporate all the changes brought in by the US team and they probably do so as well.

Most work is scoped so that I can get it resolved in my office itself, or atleast with a sense of confidence that it will be addressed by someone before I come into work the next day.
